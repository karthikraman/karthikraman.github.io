About

Systems biology, by way of Chennai, Bangalore and Zurich

I was born and schooled in Chennai, and studied Chemical Technology at ICT Mumbai before moving to IISc Bangalore for a PhD in Systems Biology, advised by Nagasuma Chandra and Saraswathi Vishveshwara. A postdoc with Andreas Wagner at the University of Zürich followed, before I returned to India in 2011 to join the Department of Biotechnology at IIT Madras.

Today I lead RamanLab at the Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI, where we combine networks, models and AI to understand, predict and manipulate complex biological systems — spanning microbiome systems biology, in silico metabolic engineering, biological network design, and biological data analysis. I also coordinate the Centre for Integrative Biology and Systems mEdicine (IBSE), teach programming and computational biology at IIT Madras, and contribute to national AI-literacy efforts such as AI Samarth.

The rest of this page is mostly the other half — the side projects, mostly around Indic computing, that I build for fun.

Projects

Active side projects

Mostly Indic computing — calendars, scripts and typesetting — plus the odd game. Some repos are private while they're still in flux; get in touch if you'd like a look.

Pañcāṅga & timekeeping

Adyātithi Private

A Pañcāṅga (Hindu luni-solar calendar) engine that powers the Adyātithi newsletter — day-to-day notes on tithi, nakshatra and festival dates.

Read on Substack →

Pañcāṅgam Public

Swiss-ephemeris–based Pañcāṅga calculator for any location and year — tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karaṇa and vāra, plus Hindu festival dates. Outputs printable monthly/daily PDFs and standard ICS calendar files.

View on GitHub →

Kamakoti Pañcāṅgam Private

A Pañcāṅga variant following Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham conventions, for a specific community's calendar and observance needs.

Ghatika Clock Public

A live web clock for traditional Indian timekeeping — divides each sunrise-to-sunrise day into 30 ghatikas (with finer subdivisions), computed from local sunrise/sunset, for ritual scheduling.

View on GitHub →

Scripts & texts

LipiSetu Private

A "bridge" (setu) for transliterating between Devanagari and other Indic scripts (lipi) — a tool for cleaner cross-script Sanskrit text.

StotraSamhita Public

A long-running effort to digitise and carefully proof-read Sanskrit stotras, and typeset them beautifully across multiple Indic scripts.
