About

Systems biology, by way of Chennai, Bangalore and Zurich

I was born and schooled in Chennai, and studied Chemical Technology at ICT Mumbai before moving to IISc Bangalore for a PhD in Systems Biology, advised by Nagasuma Chandra and Saraswathi Vishveshwara. A postdoc with Andreas Wagner at the University of Zürich followed, before I returned to India in 2011 to join the Department of Biotechnology at IIT Madras.

Today I lead RamanLab at the Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI, where we combine networks, models and AI to understand, predict and manipulate complex biological systems — spanning microbiome systems biology, in silico metabolic engineering, biological network design, and biological data analysis. I also coordinate the Centre for Integrative Biology and Systems mEdicine (IBSE), teach programming and computational biology at IIT Madras, and contribute to national AI-literacy efforts such as AI Samarth.

The rest of this page is mostly the other half — the side projects, mostly around Indic computing, that I build for fun.
